# Neon Tempo

An 80s-meets-90s drum rhythm game, made with Manaki, Mati & Kian. (Working title.)

**Play it:** https://crisai2026.github.io/neon-tempo/ (once GitHub Pages is turned on)

## Story

It's 10:00 am at the last arcade on Earth, in Nelson, New Zealand. At the back, a door is slightly open. Inside there's one machine with a sign: DO NOT TOUCH. You plug it in, and you're teleported into a world where the 80s and 90s got mixed up. To get back out, beat 3 stages before sunset (8:30 pm). Every track you play costs 1.5 hours.

- Stage 1: any 80s track (hit 50%)
- Stage 2: a 90s track (hit 60%)
- Final Machine: "Neon Tempo" (hit 65%)

## How to play

Notes slide toward a ring. Hit them in time with **D** (kick), **F** (snare), **J** (hi-hat) and **K** (crash), or tap the on-screen pads.

- Two levels: Beginner and Medium
- Five original tracks, composed in code
- Perfect / Good / Miss, combos and a x4 multiplier

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. Adjustable values live in the `CONFIG` block at the top of the script.
